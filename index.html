— FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about our pool tile and remodeling services.

How long do tile installations last?

Our premium installations typically last 15–20 years with proper maintenance. We use high-grade materials resistant to California's harsh sunlight and pool chemicals.

What areas do you serve?

We cover the entire Sacramento Valley including Sacramento, Placer, El Dorado, Yolo, and Sutter counties. Our local expertise ensures precise regional solutions.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes, we provide complimentary on-site estimates for all pool tile and remodeling projects. Our team evaluates your specific needs with no obligation.

What tile materials do you use?

We work with ceramic, porcelain, natural stone, and glass tiles. Each material is carefully selected for durability, aesthetic appeal, and performance in pool environments.

Are you licensed and insured?

Absolutely. We are fully licensed contractors with comprehensive insurance coverage. Our credentials ensure professional, reliable service for every project.

How long do installations take?

Project timelines vary based on complexity. Typical pool tile installations range from 3–7 days. We provide detailed schedules during our initial consultation.

What about pool maintenance?

We offer guidance on maintaining your new tile surfaces. Regular cleaning and proper chemical balance will extend the life of your pool tile installation.

Can you match existing designs?

Our skilled team specializes in seamless design integration. We can match or complement existing pool aesthetics with precision and creativity.

Do you handle commercial projects?

Yes, we serve both residential and commercial clients across the Sacramento Valley. Our expertise extends to hotels, resorts, and public pool facilities.

What warranty do you offer?

We provide comprehensive warranties on materials and workmanship. Specific terms are discussed during project planning to ensure your complete satisfaction.
